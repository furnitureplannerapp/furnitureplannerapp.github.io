Contact support

Email furnitureplanner.contact@gmail.com . Describe the steps that led to the issue, the expected result and what actually happened.

Helpful attachments

Include the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS or macOS version, active plan or Studio Trial, and project type: furniture or Kitchen.

A screenshot or short recording helps. Attach a project file, diagnostic information or PDF/CSV documents only when available and needed to explain the issue. For cutting issues, include the material, sheet size, kerf settings and whether calculations finished.

Reporting problems from version 1.3.0

Open “Report a problem” from the project summary or cost-estimate screen. You can contact support without attaching the project. Including project diagnostic data is optional and off by default.

When enabled, the report includes the technical furniture or kitchen configuration and definitions of the materials and equipment used, including custom items. It does not include client records, links to clients or your business profile. Project and item names, notes and other free-text project fields are omitted or neutralized. Photos, private textures and external models are not attached.

By default, the attached diagnostic project uses fictitious test prices, labor rates and margins instead of your actual amounts. The values follow fixed rules appropriate to the item type and pricing unit and are independent of your prices. The change applies only to the diagnostic copy, not to the project or library on your device.

For an issue involving your actual estimate, you can separately enable cost-estimate data (prices and rates). This option is off by default and available only together with project diagnostic data. It includes your actual prices, rates, labor costs, margins and custom estimate amounts, but does not restore excluded client data or original text. Turning off project data also turns off the inclusion of actual cost-estimate data.

Test values help with investigation, but may prevent us from reproducing an issue that depends on specific amounts, rounding or missing prices. An estimate based on test values is not your actual estimate and must not be used as a customer offer or a basis for purchases.

When you choose to contact support, the app prepares the report and opens an email with a JSON attachment or system sharing. In the latter case, choose an app that supports files. Nothing is sent automatically — you can review the message and cancel sending. If preparing the attachment fails, the app displays an error rather than silently omitting the file.

Filtering covers the diagnostic report, not the text of your message or manually added files. Review screenshots, recordings, PDFs/CSVs and regular exports before sending. Do not include unnecessary client data, passwords or full payment card details. The report retains technical identifiers and the project configuration may be confidential — the attachment is not guaranteed to be anonymous.

A diagnostic report is not a full backup. Missing original labels, private textures or library definitions that no longer exist may limit reproduction of some issues. In older app versions, a report may include the full project and prices; we recommend updating before sending.

The privacy policy explains the data scope, how requests are used and the retention rules.

Frequently asked questions

Why can prices in a diagnostic report differ from those in my project?

Unless you choose to include actual cost-estimate data, the diagnostic copy uses fictitious test values to avoid sharing your prices, rates and margins. Your original project and library are not changed. Test amounts are for troubleshooting, not for preparing an offer or ordering materials. You can choose to include actual cost-estimate data when the problem depends on specific amounts.

What if I cannot attach or send the diagnostic report?

If the app shows a preparation error, note the message or take a screenshot. An unusually large or complex report may exceed diagnostic limits. You can turn off project data and send just the problem description — you do not need to substitute a full backup.

If sharing does not transfer the file, choose another app that supports attachments or save the file to Files and attach it manually to an email. Your email provider may impose its own size limit. Opening an email or the sharing sheet does not confirm delivery.

Why can’t I see Kitchen, Visual or panel cutting?

Kitchen, the Visual plan and panel cutting are available from version 1.2.0. Update the app through the App Store and check your active plan. Available plans, trial status and remaining allowances are shown in the app.

Why can’t I save a Kitchen or open Live 3D?

Free includes 2D furniture projects and Kitchen in 2D without saving. Visual or an available Studio Trial unlocks Kitchen saving, Live 3D and AR. AR requires a supported iPhone or iPad.

Which plan includes estimates, cutting and documents?

Estimates, material lists and client PDFs are included in Premium and Studio. Panel cutting, cutting-based production estimates, production documents and CSV are Studio features. Studio Trial has additional limits.

How does Studio Trial work?

When available, you activate the trial with a button, without a purchase. It lasts 7 consecutive days and allows 3 projects in total across furniture and Kitchen. The third project does not end the trial early, and deleting projects does not restore the allowance. The trial does not automatically become a paid plan.

How many PDF and CSV files can I generate during the trial?

Each project has separate allowances for 1 quotation PDF, 1 internal calculation PDF and 1 production PDF, plus 1 CSV shared by all CSV variants. Successful file generation consumes the allowance even if you then close sharing without saving or sending.

Why are some Studio features unavailable during the trial?

The trial excludes iCloud, full backup, project import/export and kitchen material customization. It also restricts the base parameters of trial-created projects and changes to kitchen wall geometry and wall count. It is not full access to paid Studio.

What happens to an earlier trial after updating?

After updating to version 1.2.0, check the status of your earlier trial and its remaining allowances in the app. Updating is not intended to reset used trial allowances.

Why does the total after cutting differ from the earlier estimate?

Cutting accounts for sheets and waste, so its result can differ from an earlier material estimate. Check prices, currency, sheet sizes and whether the calculation covers all materials and is current. Verify completeness before exporting cutting-based documentation.

My purchase is active, but features are locked.

Check the Apple Account used for the purchase and your internet connection, then use purchase restoration in the app. Restoration applies to the subscription, not local projects. If the issue persists, send the plan and blocking message, without payment details.

Do I lose projects when the trial or subscription ends?

Projects are not automatically deleted. Entitlements come from a still-valid base plan or Free, and unavailable features are restricted. This is not a general read-only mode. Free project creation uses a limit of 3 active, non-archived projects. Offline access does not extend a subscription; online verification is required again after 30 days without verification.

Does turning off iCloud delete data or create a backup?

iCloud is off by default and requires activation, an available iCloud account and paid Premium or Studio; the trial does not include it. Turning synchronization off does not delete local or cloud data or create a backup. Do not uninstall the app without safeguarding important data.

Does “Clear data” also remove iCloud data?

No. “Clear data” removes local data, not data in iCloud. Data left in the cloud may return after synchronization runs again. Deleting an individual synchronized item also does not permanently remove all of its data from iCloud; this version of the app has no feature for permanently deleting that remaining data.

Can I turn off analytics or crash reports in the app?

No — this version of the app does not have such a switch. Analytics and crash reporting operate automatically. Sending a support request is a separate, voluntary action. The Privacy Policy explains what data is collected.

Does the app run on Mac?

Yes, the app is available from the App Store on compatible Apple Silicon Macs. AR is not available on Mac, and other features may depend on the device. Check the App Store listing for system requirements.

Where can I read the terms and cancel a subscription?

See the Terms of Use for plan features. Apple handles cancellation and billing: manage your subscription . Deleting the app alone does not stop charges.

Report processing

Data submitted in a support report is used only to analyze, reproduce and resolve the reported issue.

Sending a support request and its attachments is voluntary and requires your action. Automatic crash reporting and analytics are separate mechanisms described in the Privacy Policy .

Response time

We do our best to respond to reports as quickly as possible, but we do not guarantee a specific response time.

Response scope

Support covers app behavior, data consistency, Kitchen projects, cutting, exports and synchronization. It does not replace measurements, manufacturing decisions or document verification before production.
