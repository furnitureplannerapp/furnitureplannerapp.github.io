From design to production planning

Furniture and Kitchen

Wardrobes, built-ins, cabinets and kitchen modules: base, wall, corner and tall units, with appliances, countertops, plinths, walls and flooring.

Materials and visualization

A custom materials and fittings library is available from Free. Kitchen offers materials, handles and lighting; visualizations include scene presets and brightness control. Live 3D and AR are available from Visual on supported devices.

Estimates and documentation

Premium adds estimates and client PDFs. Studio adds panel cutting, waste and reusable offcuts, cutting-based production estimates, production documents and CSV.

Start without an app account

Free includes the 2D editor, a custom library and a limit of 3 furniture projects; creation counts active, non-archived projects. You can try Kitchen in 2D without saving. Visual unlocks unlimited saved furniture and kitchen projects. The app works on iPhone, iPad and compatible Apple Silicon Macs; AR requires a supported iPhone or iPad.

When available, Studio Trial starts without a purchase: 7 consecutive days and up to 3 created projects in total, including Kitchen. It has separate export limits and excludes features such as iCloud, full backup and project import/export. It does not automatically renew into a paid plan.
